# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in MacEmojis to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standard

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion focused on the work.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to <sharathkrml@gmail.com>. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that violate this code.
